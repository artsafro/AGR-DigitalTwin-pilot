# Hypothesis #100: type-specific DXF decoding avoids double OCS transforms

Verdict: **CONFIRMED** (synthetic fixtures only). `delivery_passed=false`.
Issue: https://github.com/artsafro/AGR-DigitalTwin-pilot/issues/100

All inputs are **synthetic** DXF drawings generated by the driver. They are not data of a real object.

## Claim

`experiments/bim-autocad-agr/experiment.py:_extract` sends POINT location, 3D POLYLINE vertex locations and
LWPOLYLINE `(x, y, elevation)` alike to `_as_wcs` (`experiments/acad-coreconsole-agr-plans/experiment.py`), which
applies OCS->WCS whenever the entity extrusion is not (0,0,1). POINT and 3D POLYLINE coordinates are already WCS
in DXF, so they are transformed a second time. A type-specific decoder (LWPOLYLINE via ezdxf's public
`vertices_in_wcs()`, POINT and 3D POLYLINE copied as WCS) removes the error and keeps LWPOLYLINE correct.

One drawing per normal: `default` (0,0,1), `neg_z` (0,0,-1), `tilted` (0,0.6,0.8). R2018, `$INSUNITS=4`, three

| Layer | Entity | Coordinates |
|---|---|---|
| P_POINT | POINT | WCS (123, 456, 789) |
| P_3D | closed 3D POLYLINE (flags 9) | WCS (100,200,300), (1100,200,300), (1100,200,1300), (100,200,1300), not rotated |

Oracle (hard-coded floats in the driver, no ezdxf/OCS/reader code): POINT and 3D = raw WCS; LWPOLYLINE =
`x*X + y*Y + 300*N` with default X=(1,0,0) Y=(0,1,0); neg_z X=(-1,0,0) Y=(0,1,0); tilted X=(-1,0,0) Y=(0,-0.8,0.6).

Serialization check (reopened files): extrusion equals the declared normal on all 9 entities; non-default normals
are present as raw group codes 210/220/230 on POINT, POLYLINE and LWPOLYLINE (default normal is omitted by ezdxf,
`hasattr("extrusion")=False`, value (0,0,1)); POLYLINE flags 9 (closed + 3D) and `is_3d_polyline=True`;
LWPOLYLINE flags 1, elevation 300, bulges and widths 0.

## Verdict and adoption scope

**CONFIRMED**: the baseline exceeds 0.01 mm by up to 3405.88 mm on non-default-normal POINT and 3D POLYLINE
entities while accepting the drawings without a diagnostic; the type-specific decoder has 0 mm error on all 27
vertices of every case, preserves every entity/kind/closure/unit/count and leaves default-normal output unchanged.

Scope limits: synthetic entities only; ezdxf as writer and reader (no native AutoCAD parity claim); no arbitrary
UCS, blocks/INSERT transforms, curves, CRS or architectural extraction tested; no runtime claim.

Adoption (separate card, as the issue says): fix `_extract` in `experiments/bim-autocad-agr/experiment.py` and the
matching decoding in `experiments/acad-coreconsole-agr-plans/experiment.py` (POINT and 3D POLYLINE copied as WCS,
LWPOLYLINE via `vertices_in_wcs()` or `_as_wcs` only for OCS entities); add `tests/test_plan_dxf_entity_frames.py`
with these three drawings and the hard-coded oracle; clarify the experiment README. Keep the existing support
contract and rejection behavior. No production CAD adapter or coordinate-frame policy follows automatically.
