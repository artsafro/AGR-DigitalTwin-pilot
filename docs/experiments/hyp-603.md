# Hypothesis #603: TOW02 centroid-unchanged faces keep triangle corners within 1 mm

**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (`delivery_passed=false`; method validity only, no delivery or placement claim).

## Claim
At least 99% of faces that the TOW02 centroid/normal matcher (pass 1 of `experiments/tow02-hand-finish-face-diff/face_diff.py`) calls unchanged also pass an independent corner check: for every selected triangle pair, `min over 6 permutations of max_k dist(a[k], b[perm[k]]) <= 0.001 m` (raw local metres). A face passes only if all its triangles pass. Rejection: any arm below 99.0%, repeat disagreement, input mutation.
