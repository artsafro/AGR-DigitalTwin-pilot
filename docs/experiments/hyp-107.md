# Hypothesis #107: unique geometric IFC wall-edge binding removes W0000 name dependence

Verdict: **CONFIRMED** (synthetic IFC fixtures only). `delivery_passed=false`.
Issue: https://github.com/artsafro/AGR-DigitalTwin-pilot/issues/107

All IFC files here are **synthetic**: the tracked pilot writer generated them from benchmark specs. None is a Revit
export or data of a real object.

## Claim

In the pilot's synthetic subset (one upright wall per slab edge), a unique geometric wall-to-edge matcher can take
the place of the `W0000..Wnnnn` wall names. Opening indices and geometry stay the same. When a binding is missing
or ambiguous, the matcher refuses the file and does not guess.

## Counterexamples and limits

- None of the 36 files (6 clean + 18 renamed + 12 malformed) broke the prediction.
- The subset is narrow by construction: one wall per contour edge, wall origin at the edge start, X axis along the
  edge, and the location line on the slab edge. Split walls, reversed axes, shifted location lines, Revit
  Axis-representation semantics and twin-floor walls were not tested. The binder refuses them (no eligible
  pair) rather than handling them.
- The generator writes the wall placement exactly at the slab vertex. Real exports with millimetre drift above 1e-4 m
  would be refused, not matched.
- Malformed detection is judged on the binder alone, so kernel errors cannot count as detection.

## Verdict

**CONFIRMED.** R1 6/6 + 18/18, R2 24/24 (max error 4.9e-15 m against the 1e-5 m limit), R3 12/12 refused with
0 accepted, R4 36/36 order/repeat-invariant. All four meet their thresholds. `delivery_passed=false`.

## Adoption scope (opt-in, experimental pilot only)

- `experiments/revit-ifc-offline-intake/wall_binding.py`: the report-only geometric binder from
  `experiments/hyp-107/geo_binder.py` (UnresolvedBinding with evidence).
- `experiments/revit-ifc-offline-intake/pilot.py`: an explicit opt-in switch (for example
  `extract(path, binding="geometric")`). The default stays the strict W0000 name mode.
- `experiments/revit-ifc-offline-intake/test_wall_binding.py`: renamed null/identical/suffix files bind to the oracle;
  shared_placement and z_shift refuse with evidence; the three wall orders give the same result; the strict name mode
  still refuses renamed files.
- Document the narrower contract: one wall per edge, unique placement match, and limits of 1e-4 m / 1e-4.
- Out of scope: a production IFC adapter location, dependency groups, generic Revit completeness, Axis semantics,
  wall splitting, CRS and customer-delivery policy.
