# DreamSim next to SSIM and the joint count (AC08)

Verdict: **no**. Do not add DreamSim as an advisory column, and do not change any gate.

The joint count already separates the accepted delivery from the known-failing one, and it sees a removed rib. DreamSim's distance overlaps between those two deliveries, and a removed rib lands inside the distance of a 1–3 px shift. SSIM ranks the failing delivery as closer to the slide.

## What the cited paper actually says

Fetched the HTML of [arXiv:2506.12563](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12563) (Wickrema et al., MITRE, "Benchmarking Image Similarity Metrics for Novel View Synthesis Applications"). It evaluates DreamSim. It is not the DreamSim training paper. DreamSim itself is Fu et al., NeurIPS 2023, their reference [2].

The abstract does say that SSIM, PSNR and LPIPS could not tell minor pixel-level changes from substantial corruption, and that DreamSim was more robust to minor defects and tracked high-level similarity.

The body is narrower than that sentence:

- On their blurred novel-view renders (section 4.1, figure 9), SSIM, LPIPS and PSNR drop after a little blur and then stay flat. DreamSim falls gradually as blur gets worse.
- On ImageNet-C (section 4.1), PSNR barely separates severities. SSIM and LPIPS move with perception but with less spread than DreamSim. DreamSim is gradual on 10 of 12 corruption types, not all of them.
- The few-pixel test (section 3.4 and 4.4, figure 12) crops about 2,000 ImageNet photos by 1, 2, 5 and 10 px. It is not a test on their rendered views. DreamSim moves the least. SSIM and PSNR drop after a few pixels.

Nothing in that paper is a facade, a joint, or a 200 px/m elevation.

## How this run was measured

Crops are 200 px/m, built with `compare_facades.panel_slide` and `panel_render`. SSIM is `methods_metrics.ssim` on the same luma weights as the methods render score (7×7 window). Higher SSIM is closer to the slide. DreamSim is package 0.2.1, type `ensemble` (DINO ViT-B/16 + CLIP ViT-B/16 + OpenCLIP ViT-B/16, LoRA), CPU, distance = `1 - cosine_similarity` from `PerceptualModel.forward` in that release. Higher DreamSim is further from the slide. Joints on a facade are `joint_count.count_joints` on the render row at 95 % of the panel field (`dark=0.85`, `tol=0.01`). The slide joint count is `slide_count`.

v010 uses the existing 400 px/m orthographic renders (resampled to 200 by `panel_render`). v007's stored PNGs are 1600×900 and have no camera block, so they were not used. v007 was re-rendered with `render_facades.py` in Blender 5.1.2 at `PX_PER_M=400` into `C:/x/issue-770/v007-renders-400`, then cropped the same way. On that readback the above-grade ribbed field is UDIM 1001 on every facade; the spec's short-facade UDIM is below grade there. The script checks that the chosen field straddles z = 0.5 m and raises if it does not.

Long facades match at 660×1332. Short facades are 660×608 (slide) and 660×607 (render); the script center-crops the extra pixel and records it. A gap above 2 px would have been an error.

Images stay in `C:/x/issue-770/`. This note commits the table, the script, and `results.json` / `results.csv` only.

## Controls

Same v010 north VPM crop, compared with itself after each edit. Shifts move the whole crop and fill the vacated edge white. The band is rows 20–55 % of the height, moved right by 0.1 m (20 px at 200 px/m). The colour zone is the central 30–70 % rectangle painted `(0.80, 0.10, 0.10)`; it also paints over the ribs on the counted row, which is why the joint count falls to 17. The missing rib is the dark run closest to centre on the 30 % row (2 px wide on this crop), painted with the neighbouring columns.

| edit | SSIM | DreamSim | crop joints |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| shift 1 px | 0.89055 | 0.000388 | 29 |
| shift 2 px | 0.80113 | 0.001688 | 29 |
| shift 3 px | 0.78802 | 0.002680 | 29 |
| band shifted 0.1 m | 0.86876 | 0.038484 | 28 |
| wrong-colour zone | 0.96186 | 0.343873 | 17 |
| missing rib | 0.99621 | 0.000905 | 28 |

A metric "separates" here only when every harmless shift stays closer than every planted defect.

- SSIM does not. A 3 px shift (0.788) is worse than the band (0.869), the colour zone (0.962) and the missing rib (0.996).
- DreamSim does not, under that rule. The missing rib (0.000905) sits between the 1 px and 2 px shifts. The band (0.038) and the colour zone (0.344) do sit well above the 3 px shift (0.0027). DreamSim tolerates the rib defect the same way it tolerates a small shift.
- The crop joint count does. Shifts stay at 29. Each planted defect changes the count.

That agrees with the paper on a few-pixel shift versus SSIM, and it does not support using DreamSim as the column that catches a facade that "looks wrong" while the joint count is the thing that actually moves.
